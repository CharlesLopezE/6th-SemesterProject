No Jet Lag

What is it about?

  • No Jet Lag is a travel assistant platform designed to help travelers reduce jet lag by generating personalized sleep schedules based on their flight details.
  • Travelers can save schedules, receive notifications during travel, and follow guided sleep patterns to adjust smoothly across time zones.
  • This system integrates a website application, using custom APIs and GPS data to optimize travel experiences.
